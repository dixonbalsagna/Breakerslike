# The launch pair's look

Owner: Art Director. 2026-10-03. The launch pair is the Protagonist and the rival (the Anti-hero). Orb's direction: no masks as the house style (they are parked for a future fighter); the unmasked faces and the silhouettes are refined; the rival is the resident "scary shiny glasses" character. Concept art, working labels, pending Legal review. Sheets are in `art/concepts/refine/` (the index is its `README.md`).

**Do they read apart in silhouette alone?** Mostly, because of a deliberate change in mass: the Protagonist is built about 20 percent broader than today with dark plated fists and shoulders, the rival about 8 percent slimmer with one long diagonal slash behind him (the tail and three flat coat plates). Both are upright humanoids and their hair trails the same way, so at 25 px the read depends on that broad round body against the slim body with the long line. If Orb wants a stronger split, the next lever is the stance (the rival straighter, the Protagonist more forward).

**Legal (RL-066) as applied to the glasses:** angular, tapered or hexagonal lenses cut asymmetrically in a thin violet frame; a dark violet tint; one hard-edged pale-violet wedge for the glare, held 0.4 s at most; no readout or scan bar on a lens; no beard; the gesture a nudge at the temple arm or the back of the forearm. The "chamfer plates" in the comparison are my reading of the brief (the plate cuts on the lead frame: bevelled corners, a heavier top plate, hinge tabs); if another reading was meant, the sheet can be redrawn.
