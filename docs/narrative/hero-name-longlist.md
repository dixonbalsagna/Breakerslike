# Hero name longlist (replacements for KAI)

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-09-28 (P0 wave 1). Draft for the EP. KAI is a no-go (Legal RL-002): it is a Dragon Ball term and it is crowded. VORR stays as the villain's working name (RL-003). Legal screens, Orb picks. Nothing here is a clearance.

## The naming rule I used

The hero is a keeper, not a conqueror. So the names are short, soft and named for what the hero keeps: land features (a lake, a wood, an upland) or words for holding, watching and mending. VORR is a hard, doubled sound. Open vowels against closed ones make the pair readable at a glance, and the two other fighters can each take a third and fourth sound family.

Steered away from: any name starting Ka-, Ki- or Go-; food and vegetable puns; short names that read as a nod to a franchise hero; and Japanese-derived names, because the genre's home language makes every sound-alike harder to clear.

The hero's gender is not decided (see `fighter-sketches.md`). All names below work for any hero.

| **Orlo** | OR-loh | humour, heroic | Italian for rim or edge. An ironic name for the hero of a world with no edge. |
| **Solan** | SOH-lan | epic, soft | The solan goose is the Scots name for a gannet, a seabird that dives from a height. Sol also hints at sun. |
| *Tarn* (flagged) | TARN | epic | My creative favourite: a still, deep mountain lake, and it fits the land rule. Rated Medium, see its row. |

## Dropped, so nobody proposes them again

- **Oren: High.** An Oren is a playable fighter in Super Dragon Ball Heroes. Direct tie.
- **Fenn:** two Star Wars characters (Fenn Signis, Fenn Rau) share the name.
- **Thane:** Thane Krios is a main character in Mass Effect.
- **Anker:** a large consumer-electronics brand.
- **Pell:** a character in the One Piece video-game material.
- **Merrow:** a sea creature that appears in several games, and a poor fit for a keeper of land.
- **Osric:** the first boss of Onimusha: Warlords (Capcom).
- **Dene:** the name of an Indigenous people in Canada. I will not use it as a fighter name.
- **Mere:** reads as the English word "mere". Weak as a name.
- **Not searched, dropped on judgement:** Lea (an exact-title Steam game), Aster (an exact-title Steam game), Ansel (a famous photographer), Aldous, Lior, Sunn, Teyo (I believe a card-game character, not verified).
