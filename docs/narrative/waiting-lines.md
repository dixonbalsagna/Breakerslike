# Waiting lines: the opponent waits while you transform

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-09-29. Draft for the EP. Names are placeholders; lines are original and unsearched.

**The canon (Orb).** A transformation is cinematic and uninterruptible, the way anime opponents traditionally let a rival transform. What can be stopped is the gauge or fill phase before it. So once a fighter's transformation reaches its cinematic phase, the opponent **waits, arms folded**. This file writes that beat for every fighter, for every opponent, in three registers: **impatient**, **polite** and **mocking**.

**How it plays.** The trigger is `opponent_transform_wait` (see `line-system.md`). The line system picks a register from the matchup default and the fighter's current mood (`register` and `shift`), and picks the line by ordered pair: the *waiter* against the *one transforming*. Lines are short, because the cinematic is the star. Each fighter keeps his or her own device: the Protagonist promises and uses contractions; the Anti-hero uses no contractions and speaks of the other in the past tense; the Empress says "we" and "petitioner"; the Cyborg is a service script.

## Notes

- **Wrong register for the matchup.** The Protagonist is grim against the world-enders, so his "polite" is curt and his "mocking" is dry, never gleeful. Against the Anti-hero, whom he is sparring, he is warm.
- **The fill phase is different.** Before the cinematic, while the gauge fills, the waiter attacks and speaks in the normal exchange lines. Waiting lines only start when the cinematic begins.
- **Alternates.** This is one line per slot. The line system can hold several alternates per slot, as the matchup cases do, once Orb has picked a direction.
