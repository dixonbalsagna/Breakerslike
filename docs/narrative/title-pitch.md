# Title pitch

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-09-29 (after Orb's tone decision in `docs/ep/vision.md`). Draft for the EP and Orb. Not a clearance.

**The brief.** Orb blended all four tones: mature, planet-scale destruction, a humorous voice, and a sincere homage to the genre. A title should hold two of those at once, so that a reader smiles and winces in the same breath. Two words that pull against each other do that best: something grand next to something wrecked.

**Self-screen (2026-09-29).** Stage 0 (no Dragon, Ball, Z, Kai, Breakers and the other franchise words; no single ordinary word). Steam and App Store search APIs for an exact title: none found for any of the eight. One quoted US web search each (`"NAME" game`). US-only, quick, not a clearance. Legal repeats stage 1.

## My top three, and why

1. **Glorious Ruin.** It is the only title that carries the whole blend in its own grammar. It also survives a comedy hero and a dark villain equally well.
2. **Splendid Wreckage.** If Orb wants the humour to lead.
3. **Skyburden.** If Orb wants the sincerity to lead. It is the easiest to own.

Pairing note: any of the top three could take "Sorry About Your Planet" as a subtitle line on the store page.

## Flags

- Nothing here is a hero or fighter name. Those wait for Legal's review of the four archetypes.
- Rejected on search: **Grudge Season** and **Sky Grudge**. "Grudge" is crowded (The Grudge, Grudge Warriors, Grudge on Steam, and more).
- Older titles in `name-longlist.md` still stand; this pitch reuses five of them (Skyburden, Elegy Circuit, Sorry About Your Planet, Ringstorm, Roundfall).
