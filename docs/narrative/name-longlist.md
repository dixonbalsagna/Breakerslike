# Game name longlist

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-09-28 (P0 wave 1). Draft for the EP. Narrative proposes, Legal screens, Orb picks, and counsel clears the final name (`docs/legal/name-screening.md`). Nothing here is a clearance.

Orb wants a completely different name from the working title. None of these contain "Meridian", "Breakers", "Calamity" or "Horizon" (Legal's notes RL-001, RL-006, RL-012).

## The shortlist for Legal (8)

These are the names I would send Legal for stages 2 to 4. All eight are rated Low in my self-screen, they cover every tone, and each is distinctive enough to own (a coined word, or a phrase with a strong second word).

| Name | Tone | Tagline idea | Why it made the cut |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skyburden** | earnest epic, dark-leaning | Power weighs what it breaks. | Pillars 4 and 5 in one word: the weight of being the one strong enough to break things. Best fit for a hero pressured by collateral. |
| **Steadfall** | earnest epic | Hold the line all the way round. | Steadfast plus fall. The hero holds while the world falls, and a fall is also how a fight round ends. |
| **Ringstorm** | earnest epic, heroic | One sky. No edges. | A storm that rings a planet: fighters circling a wrapped world. Pillar 1. |
| **Roundfall** | flexible | Every round ends on the ground. | Round is the planet and a round of a fight. Fall is night falling and a fighter falling. |
| **Elegy Circuit** | dark | It was a lovely world. | An elegy is a lament and a circuit is one loop of the planet. Ties directly to the villain's habit of mourning in advance (`fighter-sketches.md`). |
| **Roundhouse World** | heroic with humour | Hit it from every side. | Roundhouse kick plus round world. Warm and physical. |
| **Sorry About Your Planet** | comedy | Someone has to fight over it. | The hero's apology as a title. Pillars 4 and 5 played for laughs. |
| **Round Trip Ruin** | comedy, dark | Fly far enough and you hit what you broke. | Alliterative, and states the wrapped-planet joke: the damage is waiting where you started. |

My own order if Orb asks for a lean: **Skyburden**, **Elegy Circuit**, **Steadfall** for a serious game; **Sorry About Your Planet** if Orb picks comedy. Tone is still pending from Orb, so the shortlist spans it.

## How I screened

- **Stage 0 (my check, all names).** No Dragon, Ball, Z, Saiyan, Kai, Kaio, Kame, Namek, Budokai, Tenkaichi, Xenoverse, FighterZ, Sparking, Kakarot or Breakers. Also avoided, as sound-alikes or habits of the franchise: orb, sphere and globe (synonyms of "ball"), "Super ___", "Ultimate", "Zero", "Raging", "Burst", "Legends", "Infinite", food or vegetable puns, and any name starting Ka- or Ki-. No single ordinary word standing alone, except where noted in the appendix. Every name below passed unless its row says otherwise.
- **Stage 1 (my quick version, all names), run 2026-09-28:**
  - Steam store search API for the exact title (games and software), US store.
  - App Store (iTunes) search API for an exact-title app, US store.
  - One quoted web search per name (`"NAME" game`) with a US-only search tool.
  - "Steam 0" means the search returned no results at all for the word. "iOS no exact" means no app with that exact title in the first 25 results.
- **Not done (Legal's stages 2 to 4).** Trademark registers, domains and handles, meanings in other languages, Google Play, console stores, itch.io direct search, and non-US results.
- **Rating scale** is Legal's (`review-log.md`): Low, Medium, High. I applied it to games, apps and brands only, not to ordinary words. The four High names are in the appendix, not the table.

Tone tags: **Epic** = earnest epic. **Humour** = heroic with humour. **Comedy**. **Dark**.

## Notes for Legal

- Every name here needs stage 1 repeated by Legal. Mine is a quick screen, US-only, and can miss things.
- Ordinary-phrase names (Fly Either Way, Either Way Round, All the Way Round, No Corners, Pardon the Dust, Mind the Crater) will be hard to search for and hard to protect even where nothing collides. I kept them because Orb has not said whether a comedy tone is wanted.
- Two flags to judge: **Please Fight Elsewhere** (shares a joke with a Dragon Ball mod title) and **Fists Across the Sky** (the word "Fist" calls up Fist of the North Star).
- Search-tool limit: the web search is US-only, and a first pass of subagent searches hit a usage limit and returned nothing. Every web result above was re-run by me directly the same day.
