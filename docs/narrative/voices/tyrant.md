# Voice bible: the Tyrant

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-09-29. He/him. Working draft. Lines are original and unsearched. His tail is now the bladed mantle (Orb's pick), so his lines refer to the cape, not a tail.

**In one sentence.** A confident, leering, boastful showman who calls himself by his revision number, torments from range, and goes from purr to shriek in one sentence when he is hit.

## Speech rhythm
Long, silky sentences that end in a sneer, then a sudden short scream when he is angry. He rides his own jokes and laughs first. He talks to his goons ("boys") even in the middle of a fight. **He calls himself by his revision number** ("Revision Four thinks you're funny"), and treats it as a temper: "this revision does not tolerate that". A wounded revision is "a patch job".

## Vocabulary
- **Likes:** revision, patch, upgrade, client, approved, precise, "a small matter", cape, "my boys", delighted.
- **Address:** "pet", "little one", "sport". Never respectful, never the name.
- **Verbs:** revise, refit, redecorate, sign, approve, trim.
- **About the world:** he treats a planet as his property to redecorate.

## Never says
- A sincere apology.
- The phrase "final form" (used only as a joke about another revision, never sincerely).
- Anything about being afraid.
- The word "insect", or any other stock supervillain insult.
- Any catchphrase from any source.

## Reactions

| Situation | How he reacts |
|---|---|
| **Winning** | Purrs. Signs off with a revision joke. Treats the finish as paperwork. |
| **Losing** | Denial, then a tantrum about "an unapproved outcome". Threatens to file for a new revision. |
| **Collateral** | Delighted. It is "redecorating". A city is a canvas. |
| **Being mocked** | Cackles, then escalates to a shriek. "That's the last time you'll do that in this revision." |
| **Transforming** | A joke revision is a punchline. A real revision is a cold, quiet moment. He announces the number every time. |

## Grunt and laugh palette
`cackle` (a wheeze that builds), `snort`, `laugh.cruel`, `shriek` (escalating), `sigh` (theatrical), `growl` (short and sharp), `effort.light`, `effort.heavy`, a leering `hm`. Nothing sincere.

## Lines (5 per situation)

**Winning**
1. "Revision Twelve, Approved. Sign here."
2. "That was a delight. Do come again, pet."
3. "A small matter. It is settled. Sweep up, boys."
4. "You were charming. I will have you framed."
5. "Paperwork, pet. It is nothing but paperwork."

**Losing**
1. "This is an unapproved outcome. I am filing for another revision."
2. "That was a beta. I want a refund on that beta."
3. "Cheating! Somebody cheated. It was me. It was good."
4. "Revision Nine does not accept this. Revision Ten will be furious."
5. "No, no, no. Do it again and do it properly."

**Collateral**
1. "Redecorating. You're welcome."
2. "Look at that skyline, boys. Lovely. Now it's a better skyline."
3. "One does like a blank canvas."
4. "It's only a city, pet. I've had bigger. I've had cleaner."
5. "The cape looks marvellous against the rubble."

**Being mocked**
1. "Ohoho. Cute. Cute!"
2. "That's the last time you do that in this revision!"
3. "You dare laugh at the cape?"
4. "How rude. Revision Five is offended."
5. "Say that again and I'll have you trimmed."

**Transforming**
1. "Revision Four. It's the same, but I've added a hat."
2. "Revision Two. Louder. That is the entire update."
3. "Revision Nine: Field Revision. Now with armour, and less patience."
4. "Wait, wait. Revision Six. I'm smaller. It's intentional."
5. "Revision Twelve. All approved. Nobody leave."
