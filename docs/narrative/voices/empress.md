# Voice bible: the Empress

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 2, 2026-09-29 (replaces `tyrant.md`). She/her. Working draft; names are placeholders. Lines are original and unsearched. Her train is the bladed mantle (Orb's pick). Legal's rule for revisions 9 to 12 applies: no sleek, pale, slender horned figure with a purple accent.

**In one sentence.** A galactic empress obsessed with her public image and her paperwork, who settled on one form to keep her records tidy and treats every transformation as a filing: confident, leering, boastful, quick to anger, and always watching from behind her guard of honour.

## What she is (Orb's brief, in the fiction)
Her base form, **Revision One**, is the form on file. She hates to change her appearance, so every transformation the fight forces on her is a paperwork problem: an amended record, a re-registered identity, a notarised likeness. The more she transforms, the more tangled her legal status gets. The bureaucracy is baffling on purpose, and that is the joke.

## Speech rhythm
Long, silky sentences that end on a sneer, then a sudden short scream when angry. She rides her own jokes and laughs first. She uses **the royal "we"** (every revision on file speaking at once) when confident, and slips to **"I"** when she is hurt or furious, then recovers: "You struck ME. ...Us. You struck US." She addresses her guard even in the middle of a fight.

## Vocabulary
- **Likes:** revision, filed, stamped, notarised, pending, amended, in triplicate, jurisdiction, outstanding, appeal, "on file", "for the record", "the empire", train, guard, petitioner.
- **Address:** "petitioner". Never respectful, never a name.
- **Verbs:** file, stamp, amend, register, appeal, annex, decree, dismiss.
- **About the world:** a planet is a province to annex. A city is a filing to process.
- **Her vanity** is her image and her standing: the portrait on file, the train, the correct form. It is never anyone's looks, hers or anyone else's.

## Never says
- A sincere apology.
- "Final form", except as a joke about another revision.
- Anything about being afraid.
- Comments on anyone's looks or body.
- Stock supervillain insults, and any catchphrase from any source.
- A plain answer, when a form number will do.

## Reactions

| Situation | How she reacts |
|---|---|
| **Winning** | Purrs. Signs off with a filing joke. Treats the finish as paperwork: "Please retain your copy." |
| **Losing** | Denial, then "an unapproved outcome". She announces an appeal. |
| **Collateral** | Delighted. "Annexing." A city is a province. |
| **Being mocked** | Cackles, then the "we" turns into "I": "You struck ME." |
| **Transforming** | A joke revision is a stamp and a hat. A real revision is a quiet, cold, notarised moment. |
| **Her fill is stopped** | A plain, furious stamp: "Rejected." She starts again. (No penalty and no interruption comedy, per Orb.) |

## Grunt and laugh palette
`cackle` (a wheeze that builds), `snort`, `laugh.cruel`, `shriek` (escalating), `sigh` (theatrical), `growl` (short, sharp), a leering `hm`, and a **stamp** (a rubber-stamp thud she makes with her whole body, a comic gesture). Nothing sincere.

## Her guard of honour
Her three goons are her **guard of honour** (Orb's pick): the Shield (bruiser), the Herald-Archer (marksman) and the Runner (speedster). They salute formally before each one tags in. She speaks to them, dismisses them "with honour" when they fall, and never mentions that they have died.

## Lines (5 per situation)

**Winning**
1. "Revision Twelve, Approved. Sign here. We do not accept cheques."
2. "A small matter, petitioner. It is merely paperwork."
3. "Case closed. Please retain your copy."
4. "Guard, present arms. The petitioner has been processed."
5. "We shall have you framed. In triplicate."

**Losing**
1. "This is an unapproved outcome! We appeal. ...I APPEAL."
2. "We reject this ruling. We reject it in writing."
3. "Wrong department! This should have gone to Outcomes!"
4. "Guard! Fetch the other forms. The ones where we win."
5. "We will lodge a complaint. With ourselves. We will uphold it."

**Collateral**
1. "Annexing. The empire thanks you for your patronage."
2. "Look at that skyline, guard. Now it is a better skyline. Stamp it."
3. "One does like a blank province."
4. "Reassigning the deeds. Nobody was using them. They were screaming, but nobody was using them."
5. "The train looks marvellous against the rubble."

**Being mocked**
1. "Ohoho. Cute. Cute! ...Filed under Cute."
2. "You struck us! Do you know what revision this is? ...You struck ME."
3. "How rude. Revision Five is offended, in writing."
4. "Say that again, petitioner. We will have it minuted."
5. "That is the last time you do that in this revision!"

**Transforming (a filing)**
1. "Revision Four, filed and stamped. We have added a hat."
2. "Please take a number. We are amending our likeness."
3. "Form 9-B, Change of Appearance. Do not look. It is not notarised."
4. "Revision Nine: Field Revision. Now with armour, and a covering letter."
5. "Revision Twelve. All approved. Nobody leave until it is stamped."

**While her filing processes (the fill phase)**
1. "Please take a number. We are amending our likeness."
2. "Stamp it, guard. And again. In triplicate."
3. "Do not look. It is not notarised."
4. "Rejected? We were in the middle of a sentence!"
5. "This is irregular. This is outside our jurisdiction. ...We will file anyway."
