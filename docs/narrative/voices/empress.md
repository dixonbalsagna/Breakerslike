# Voice bible: the Empress

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 3, 2026-09-29. She/her. Working draft; names are placeholders. Lines are original and unsearched. Her train is the bladed mantle (Orb's pick). Legal's rule for revisions 9 to 12 applies: no sleek, pale, slender horned figure with a purple accent.

**Orb's rule for her paperwork: it is diegetic only.** There are no stamp cards, no visible forms, and no REJECTED or REGISTERED text. The paperwork lives in **her voice** (dread of the mountain of filing she will face later, complaints about her legal status, muttered form numbers) and in **in-world clues** her body and her guard give off.

**In one sentence.** A galactic empress obsessed with her public image, who settled on one form to keep her records tidy and dreads every transformation because of the paperwork it will cost her later: confident, leering, boastful, quick to anger, and always watching from behind her guard of honour.

## What she is
Her base form, **Revision One**, is the form on file. She hates to change her appearance, so every transformation the fight forces on her is a bureaucratic disaster that she will have to sort out afterwards. The more she transforms, the more tangled her legal status gets, and the more she talks about it. The bureaucracy is baffling on purpose, and it is heard, never shown.

## Speech rhythm
Long, silky sentences that end on a sneer, then a sudden short scream when angry. She rides her own jokes and laughs first. She uses **the royal "we"** (every revision on file speaking at once) when confident, and slips to **"I"** when hurt or furious, then recovers: "You struck ME. ...Us. You struck US." She addresses her guard even in the middle of a fight, and mutters form numbers under her breath.

## Vocabulary
- **Likes:** revision, on file, on the record, off the record, jurisdiction, outstanding, appeal, "in triplicate", "the mountain of paperwork", "later", the empire, train, guard, petitioner.
- **Address:** "petitioner". Never respectful, never a name.
- **Verbs:** file, amend, register, annex, decree, dismiss, complain.
- **About the world:** a planet is a province to annex. A city is a filing to process.
- **Her vanity** is her image and her standing: the portrait on file, the train, the correct form. It is never anyone's looks, hers or anyone else's.

## Never says
- A sincere apology.
- "Final form", except as a joke about another revision.
- Anything about being afraid.
- Comments on anyone's looks or body.
- Stock supervillain insults, and any catchphrase from any source.
- A plain answer, when a form number will do.

## Reactions

| Situation | How she reacts |
|---|---|
| **Winning** | Purrs. Signs off with a filing joke. Treats the finish as paperwork: "Please retain your copy." |
| **Losing** | Denial, then "an unapproved outcome". She announces a complaint. |
| **Collateral** | Delighted. "Annexing." A city is a province. |
| **Being mocked** | Cackles, then the "we" turns into "I": "You struck ME." |
| **Before a revision** | A weary sigh, a glance at her aide, a muttered form number, and she smooths her regalia. |
| **In a revision** | Grumbles about the paperwork she will face later, in the middle of the cinematic. |
| **Her fill is stopped** | Furious, plain: "Again? From the top." No text, no penalty. |

## Diegetic clues (what the player sees and hears instead of forms)
- **The guard's protocol gestures.** Each new stage of a filing makes the guard snap a stiff, formal gesture in sequence. Three gestures means the revision is approved. It is the fill gauge, read in bodies.
- **She smooths her regalia** before a revision, a fastidious ritual that telegraphs it.
- **The pained aide.** One guard member, the aide, winces, checks a wristwatch and sighs. His posture worsens with each revision, and the tangle shows in his face.
- **The muttering.** Form numbers under her breath, growing as the fight goes on.
- **The sigh of dread.** A long exhale and rolled shoulders, like someone lifting a heavy box. Relief afterwards.
- **The guard updates the salute.** After a revision the guard changes its salute (a small hat gesture, for the hat). Someone salutes the old way and gets a glare.

## Grunt and laugh palette
`cackle` (a wheeze that builds), `snort`, `laugh.cruel`, `shriek` (escalating), `sigh` (theatrical), `sigh.heavy` (the weary sigh of someone facing a mountain of paperwork), `growl` (short, sharp), a leering `hm`, and `mutter` (form numbers, indistinct). Nothing sincere.

## Her guard of honour
The Shield (bruiser), the Herald-Archer (marksman) and the Runner (speedster), plus an aide. They salute formally before each one tags in. She dismisses them "with honour" when they fall, and never mentions that they have died.

## Lines (5 per situation)

**Winning**
1. "Revision Twelve, Approved. Sign here. We do not accept cheques."
2. "A small matter, petitioner. It is merely paperwork."
3. "Case closed. Please retain your copy."
4. "Guard, present arms. The petitioner has been processed."
5. "We shall have you framed. In triplicate."

**Losing**
1. "This is an unapproved outcome! We appeal. ...I APPEAL."
2. "We reject this ruling. We reject it in writing."
3. "Wrong department! This should have gone to Outcomes!"
4. "Guard! Fetch the other outcomes. The ones where we win."
5. "We will lodge a complaint. With ourselves. We will uphold it."

**Collateral**
1. "Annexing. The empire thanks you for your patronage."
2. "Look at that skyline, guard. Now it is a better skyline."
3. "One does like a blank province."
4. "Reassigning the deeds. Nobody was using them. They were screaming, but nobody was using them."
5. "The train looks marvellous against the rubble."

**Being mocked**
1. "Ohoho. Cute. Cute! ...Filed under Cute."
2. "You struck us! Do you know what revision this is? ...You struck ME."
3. "How rude. Revision Five is offended, and will say so in writing."
4. "Say that again, petitioner. We will have it minuted."
5. "That is the last time you do that in this revision!"

**While her filing processes (the fill phase, voice only)**
1. "Nine-B. Nine-C. Where is nine-C?" (a mutter)
2. "Do you have any idea how much paperwork this is? ...Never mind. Hold still."
3. "We will deal with the forms later. We will deal with the forms for a month."
4. "Guard, if this ever goes on the record, you saw nothing."
5. "A revision. Now. In the middle of a fight. The forms alone."
6. If the fill is stopped: "Again? From the top."

**Transforming (the cinematic, voice only)**
1. "Revision Four. We have added a hat. Do not ask what that will cost us in paperwork."
2. "Revision Nine: Field Revision. Armour. The covering letter alone is forty pages."
3. "Revision Twelve. And every one of you will be sorry, once we have finished the filing."
4. "There. A new likeness. Somebody will have to certify it. It will be us. It is always us."
5. "Revision Two. Louder. That is the entire update."

**The fold (a complaint with no mechanical effect)**
1. "This is irregular. This is outside our jurisdiction. ...We will file anyway."
2. "No clerks. No stationery. We will complain about this later."
3. "A private room. How very irregular. Guard, note the room."
4. "Everything we do out here will need a form. We will do it anyway."
5. "Nobody to annex. And nobody to complain to. How dull."

**Comeback (the Rally, voice only)**
1. "We will deal with the forms later."
2. "Off the record. Guard, you did not see that."
3. "Guard, close ranks. We are not receiving visitors."
4. "This session is adjourned. Refreshments, guard."
5. "We are not hurt. We are pending."
