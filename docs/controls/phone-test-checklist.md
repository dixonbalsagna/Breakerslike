# Phone test checklist (for Orb)

About 15 minutes. Do it on a real phone, in the browser you actually use. Open https://dixonbalsagna.github.io/orb-combat-ex/play/ . Keep the game's own **Send feedback** button for anything odd: it tells us the seed and your setup.

Write down before you start: the phone model, the browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox), and whether the phone is old or new.

**Good to know first**
- You play the left fighter (KAI). The right one is the computer.
- A tap does nothing if the computer's attack is already running, or for a moment after a move ends. That is expected for now and is not a fault. If a tap seems ignored, wait a second and try again.
- A fresh match ignores attacks for its first half second.
- Tap, hold and swipe on **Attack** mean light, heavy and signature (the signature needs 45 charge).

## 1. Landscape basics (4 minutes)

Turn the phone sideways. Tick each when it works.

| Do this | You should see |
| :--- | :--- |
| Put your left thumb on the left side of the screen and drag | a ring appears under your thumb and the fighter flies that way |
| Flick the same thumb quickly to one side, then let go | the fighter lunges, and the top-left chip shows DODGE for a moment |
| Drag your thumb far out past the ring and hold | the ring gets heavier and the fighter flies faster |
| Hold **Guard** | the chip shows GUARD; let go and it goes back to PRESS |
| Hold **Power** | a ring shows on the button and the CHARGE bar fills |
| Tap **Attack** | a quick attack starts (a line appears in the feed if it is on) |
| Hold **Attack** for half a second | a heavier attack |
| Swipe **Attack** upward, quickly | the signature (needs the charge) |

## 2. Several fingers (3 minutes)

| Do this | You should see |
| :--- | :--- |
| Move with the left thumb and tap **Attack** with the right | both work at once |
| Hold **Guard** with one right finger and tap **Attack** with another | the chip stays GUARD and the attack still goes |
| Hold **Power** and move with the left thumb | you charge while flying |
| Rest the heel of your hand on the screen edge while playing | note whether it makes anything fire by accident (we have not added palm rejection yet) |
| Use three fingers at once on purpose | no button sticks on after you let go |

If a button stays "stuck" after you let go, note exactly what you were doing.

## 3. Left-handed (2 minutes)

Turn on **Left-handed** in the game's options (if you cannot find it, tell the EP and we will point you to it). Then check:
- the buttons moved to the left and the move area to the right;
- the thumbs can reach everything without covering the fighters;
- everything in section 1 still works.

## 4. Portrait (2 minutes)

Hold the phone upright.
- Are all three buttons on screen, big enough to hit, and clear of the plates at the top?
- Can you still see both fighters?
- Does flicking, guarding and attacking still work?

Portrait is the fallback; sideways is the intended way to play. Tell us if portrait is unplayable.

## 5. Does it feel instant? (2 minutes)

No tools needed: tap **Guard** and watch the chip.
- It should change **as soon as your finger lands**. Say "instant", "a little late" or "clearly late".
- Do the same with a flick.

If you want a number, use a second phone to film in **slow motion** (240 frames per second) with your finger and the screen in view. Count the frames from the finger touching to the button filling. **36 frames or fewer** is inside the 150 ms budget; send us the clip if it is more.

## 6. Browser interference (2 minutes)

Try to break it:
- long-press **Attack**, **Guard** and the move area for five seconds: no menu, no text selection, no magnifier;
- swipe down from the top of the screen during play (it may open the phone's own shade, which is fine) and check the page does not refresh or scroll;
- swipe in from the left or right edge: the browser should not go back;
- turn the phone from sideways to upright and back: the game should keep running and the buttons should reappear in the right places;
- lock the screen and unlock it: nothing should be stuck.

## What to send back

For each section: pass, or what went wrong, in a sentence. Please include the phone model and browser, a screenshot or screen recording of anything odd, and, if the game let you, the **Send feedback** report.

If it feels late, say whether it is the tap or the fighter's reaction that is slow: they have different causes.
