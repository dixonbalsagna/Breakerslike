### The lock-break row (GB-008)

c1714b5, default arm, seed 46, ticks 34043 to 35408 (525.35 to 547.23 s): both fighters broke lock on the same tick; fighter 1 was launched 1.53 s in; `launch.gd:316` cleared its hidden flag without `regainLock`, so no `found` event and no 6 s no-rebreak guard, and the harness episode stayed open until the next `found`. `fighter.gd:319` (a charge) clears it the same way. ### Why E4 and E5 never finish (GB-009)

The brink fighter was never finished because no decisive shot ever landed. A barrage needs four clean bolts inside 90 ticks (`interrupts.json` `blast.barrage`: `enderAfter` 4, `window` 90); a charged shot decides only at full charge (`knockFull`, a tap is 0.6). The tapper fired one press every 24 idle ticks in the mix L L H: bolts at 0, 24, 72 and 96 ticks, so never four inside 90, and the H was a tapped heavy. No `brink_open` or `finisher_start` ever appeared in the trace (the melee player went to the brink at 222.7 s and then flew off; the blaster hit it about 2,100 times a match, 72,000 damage). E1 finishes because a bolt every 8 ticks closes a barrage in about 24 ticks. It is the script's cadence meeting a rule that reads a cadence, not the finisher itself; Game Design's section 21 (any shot counts, window 120) and a quicker blaster script (idle 14 ticks, mix L L L H) are the two fixes, and E3 to E5 now use the quicker script.
