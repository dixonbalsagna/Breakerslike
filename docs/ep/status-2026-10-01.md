# Status for Orb's return, 2026-10-01

Written by the EP while Orb was away. Live build: https://dixonbalsagna.github.io/orb-combat-ex/play/ (commit c1714b5 and later docs).

## Live now

- **Local two-player versus.** A second controller or the keyboard joins with any button (T on the keyboard also works). A prompt shows on player two's side. The pause menu has "Player two: hand back to the AI". Each player has their own controller layout.
- **Controllers:** three layouts, remapping, a pause menu that works from a pad.
- **Combat:** the new attack families (clean hits, clipped blows, clean dodges), hits that land within arm's reach, a parry that ends the exchange, the dodge as a physical step-around, skid-first launches (DRIVE DOWN) with a rarer CRATER SLAM.
- **Agency (step 3):** the perfect block with a counter-attack, the dodge-cancel, the burst, the reversal, attacks going stale, three AI levels, with their own poses (perfect block, reversal, dodge-cancel, burst). A light masher beats easy, wins about 40% against medium and rarely beats hard.
- **Pacing:** three transformations at about 1:45, 3:40 and 5:30, short pauses, the power-up landing on the visual break. Signatures on a 120 s cooldown.
- **Joint limits:** knees and elbows can no longer bend the wrong way; limits are enforced in the rig for every pose, blend and the ragdoll (0 frames past a limit in 24,000 scanned, was 32%).
- **Animation overhaul:** an active ragdoll, hit reactions computed from each blow, per-fighter ragdoll shapes, feet on slopes, blow weight, cloth, defender bracing, idle personality, flight poses, battle damage on the body.
- **World:** crater rims a fighter can ride up as ramps, gentler rubble heaps, wider structure damage at tiers 3 and 4. Fighters bounce, tumble and launch off crater lips, ridges and heaps (about 1.3 lip launches a minute), with ragdoll poses, dust and debris.
- **Presentation:** a guard arc in front of a guarding fighter, transformation shots and effects, the charging aura, world reactions at tier 3 (rubble, cracks, windows, the sky), speed lines, panel cut-ins, the winner in the wreckage, face cut-ins with placeholder portraits, torn clothing and scuffs.

- On about 5% of strikes the attacker moves a long way in one frame to catch a launched body.
- A shared keyboard can drop keys when both players hold many at once. Shift mashing can trigger Windows Sticky Keys. Firefox may open Quick Find on player two's `/` key.

- The attacker can cancel free before his wind-up, and the stick picks his arrival side.
- Energy is hold, not toggle (a setting keeps the toggle). A provisional Escape control exists on R3 (keys C, X, Quote; swipe up on Guard on touch) but nothing answers it yet. The Brawler layout's transform is now D-pad up, held.
- The design for the rest: docs/design/agency-pass.md (range bands, the taunt as a challenge, two charges, the alchemist with timing, the traded-blows set piece, energy, boost and escape, buried in the crater). Build order: the exchange decided at the wind-up, then energy blasts.
- Not yet built: everything else in that doc. Signature cooldown is still 120 s until the energy slice.
- Power shown without speed: levitating rocks at tiers 3 and 4; craters throw a shock ring, rim chunks and dust scaled by the causer's tier, including the transformation's crater. Pressure rings on dashes are built but not drawn: the VFX session's permission check refused the edit, and it waits for Orb.
- A tumble down a slope never exceeds 1.25 times its first-contact speed (the mountain tumble Orb found odd).
- The exchange starts at the wind-up: a press beyond 3 body heights starts only the attacker's approach (a lunge from mid range, a flight from far) and the defender is free until he arrives. Until the taunt and the held charge are built, a far tap flies in.
- Split-screen camera: the jolt when a player is knocked away is fixed (the split opens fast), with zoom pulsing and divider jumps removed; the full camera sweep passes on the newest sim.
- Skids run twice as long (a body used to launch off its own trench: a defect), furrows are deeper by speed and tier, tumbles carve the ground, a skid ends in a ploughed ridge. Walls now end 21.5% of launches (band 5 to 15): with Game Design.
- Buried in the crater: the hardest slams (a crater 1.75 body heights deep, or a special at 1.5) leave the fighter in the bowl for a second, about twice a match. The attacker gets one unanswerable heavy blow within 40 ticks (from any distance; a stand-in exchange until Combat authors one); the buried fighter can guard and burst out from tick 40. Walls end 15.2% of launches (was 22%).
- Projectiles are in the sim with Game Design's first numbers, dormant until the energy slice fires them.
- The animation check passes again (the broken-arm test is now a controlled case); the ragdoll stays inside the joint limits by itself on most frames.
- The far taunt is a challenge (the rival's attack press answers it: both rush and meet); a held light is a fast charge that can be feinted, a held heavy a committed one that earns a launch; the far band starts beyond 12.5 body heights. A patient player starts 44 to 49% of exchanges against a masher.
- Fixed in 7408870: a light-only player can finish a fight. A blur closes with its own knock-back after four landed lights, knock-backs count as decisive, and the AI uses the launch earners less at lower levels. A masher beats easy 50 of 50, medium 40 of 100, hard 2 of 50; two lights-only players finish 40 of 40 (median 2:41); brink to KO 66 s; median match 7:25. Launches between two AIs are 20.5% of decided exchanges (target 30); the medium AI is softer; lights-only fights end fast (brink 26 s).
- Energy blasts: hold Energy and press light for bolts (1 ki, taps make a volley) or heavy for a charged shot (8 ki, charges while held, fires on release), from any range, with nobody locked. A dodge lets a shot pass, a fresh guard press sends it back, a held guard takes it. Any blast stops a light charge; a heavy charge shrugs off bolts. The signature limit is provisional and behind a switch (data/director/interrupts.json signature.provisional): on = 15 s and 0.6 damage (5.6 signatures a match between AIs, structures lost 41.7%), off = 120 s (3.8 a match, 32.3%).
