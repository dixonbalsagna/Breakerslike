# Status for Orb's return, 2026-10-01

Written by the EP while Orb was away. Live build: https://dixonbalsagna.github.io/orb-combat-ex/play/ (commit 45e406e).

## Live now

- **Local two-player versus.** A second controller or the keyboard joins with any button (T on the keyboard also works). A prompt shows on player two's side. The pause menu has "Player two: hand back to the AI". Each player has their own controller layout.
- **Controllers:** three layouts, remapping, a pause menu that works from a pad.
- **Combat:** the new attack families (clean hits, clipped blows, clean dodges), hits that land within arm's reach, a parry that ends the exchange, the dodge as a physical step-around, skid-first launches (DRIVE DOWN) with a rarer CRATER SLAM.
- **Agency (step 3):** the perfect block with a counter-attack, the dodge-cancel, the burst, the reversal, attacks going stale, three AI levels, with their own poses (perfect block, reversal, dodge-cancel, burst). A light masher beats easy, wins about 40% against medium and rarely beats hard.
- **Pacing:** three transformations at about 1:45, 3:40 and 5:30, short pauses, the power-up landing on the visual break. Signatures on a 120 s cooldown.
- **Animation overhaul:** an active ragdoll, hit reactions computed from each blow, per-fighter ragdoll shapes, feet on slopes, blow weight, cloth, defender bracing, idle personality, flight poses, battle damage on the body.
- **World:** crater rims a fighter can ride up as ramps, gentler rubble heaps, wider structure damage at tiers 3 and 4. The lip launch itself is not live yet (it is in World's next slices).
- **Presentation:** a guard arc in front of a guarding fighter, transformation shots and effects, the charging aura, world reactions at tier 3 (rubble, cracks, windows, the sky), speed lines, panel cut-ins, the winner in the wreckage, face cut-ins with placeholder portraits, torn clothing and scuffs.

## Not tested by anyone

- Real controllers. Rendering used simulated gamepads in Chrome and Edge. The 12-step checklist is in docs/controls/local-two-player.md (plus checks 13 and 14).
- A real phone, and the old-laptop benchmark at /bench/.
- The web frame cost of the animation overhaul (benchmarks were taken on a busy machine).

## Known rough edges on the live build

- On about 5% of strikes the attacker moves a long way in one frame to catch a launched body.
- KAI's win rate is about 58% in AI-against-AI play since step 3; QA is measuring the correction.
- A shared keyboard can drop keys when both players hold many at once. Shift mashing can trigger Windows Sticky Keys. Firefox may open Quick Find on player two's `/` key.

## Balance (QA, 3,200 matches on 738add7, the contact build)

KAI 51.7%, median match 7:09, civilians lost 16.1%, front-row structures 28.1%; 73 bands pass, 7 fail; hard tests 7 of 7. Strikes out of reach: 46 of 889,593.

## Rulings the EP made in Orb's absence (overrule any)

- The last stand gives one free signature; its 20 s start when the fighter is next free.
- Fighters start 900 units apart (was 750), for the entrance craters.
- Every spoken line gets a face cut-in (UI had capped it at six a minute).
- Crater lips: 0.5 of the radius wide, a crest at 0.40 of the depth.
- Structure reach at tiers 3 and 4 starts at ×1.6 and ×2.8.
- Remapping: Pause can't be rebound; shared-keyboard halves can't share a key; the mode button can be rebound; Start on an unused pad joins.
- The dodge-and-counter blink became a physical step-around.
