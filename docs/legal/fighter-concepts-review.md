# Fighter concepts: IP review

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-09-29. Reviews the four fighter archetypes in `docs/ep/vision.md` against Orb's line, "staples yes, signatures no". Franchise elements are named only as far as needed to make each call. Not legal advice. Have a lawyer look at the final designs before a store page or trailer.

## How the sorting works

- **Staple (keep):** an idea, mechanic or convention shared by many games and shows, such as flight, energy blasts, tiered power-ups, rivalry, henchmen, a weak-point core. Anyone may use it.
- **Signature (replace):** a specific device, look, name, object or story beat that people link to one franchise. Using it, even with a new label, reads as copying.
- **Grey:** could go either way. Orb decides, and the default is to replace.
- **The strip test:** remove the labels and colours and describe the device in one sentence. If a fan of the franchise would still say "that's the one from the show", it is a signature. A sincere, respectful homage does not change the test. Neither does calling the copy a parody: parody defences are narrow and untested for a game like this.

## Verdicts

| Fighter | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Protagonist | CONDITIONAL | The character is staples. The item-collecting wish, the beast stage, the hair change and the named multiplier stage are signatures. |
| 2. Anti-hero | CONDITIONAL | The rival is a staple. Two fighters fusing through an item thrown from one to the other is a signature beat. |
| 3. Galactic tyrant | CONDITIONAL | Minions, tail and cutting beams are staples. A many-form emperor whose forms echo the franchise's is a signature. |
| 4. Demon cyborg | CONDITIONAL | Consuming, molting and portals are staples. Beam-turns-people-into-food, absorbing a sidekick for the final form, and a spherical core are signatures. |

Each becomes GO once the replacements below are chosen and the final designs pass the checklist in `originality-rules.md`.

## Notes across all four

- **A combination of two villains is still two sets of signatures.** Mixing two franchise villains does not make the result original. Every mechanism above was picked to stand alone.
- **Names.** The placeholder gloves name in Orb's notes must not go into a committed file or a build. Narrative names every item, form and move, and Legal screens each one.
- **Designs.** Each finished design goes through the checklist in `originality-rules.md`, above all the silhouette and three-colour test.
- **AI concept art.** Allowed by Orb. Never prompt with franchise names, characters or images (`licence-recommendation.md`, section 8). Keep the prompts.
- **Platform content rules.** The consuming-people beat and mature violence may affect store content ratings, and mobile store rules in particular. We have not checked. Confirm before submission.
