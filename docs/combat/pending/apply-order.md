## 3. New keys for Tools

**`combat-recipes.schema.json`** (parked in `docs/tools/pending/apply-recipes.cjs`):
- The fighter key `rival` replaces `antihero`. One self-test case names the old pointer: `pool-not-for-fighter` expects `/pools/antihero`.
- `showcase` (optional, top level): `_about`, and fighter to a list of rows. A row: `id` (`showcase.<name>`), `strike` (a strike id), `sends` (across, up, down or turned), `clearAboveBh` (optional, a number over 0), `status` (live, posed or waiting). Cross-checks: every fighter of `pools` has a list (it may be empty); a row's `strike` is in one of that fighter's pools; no id twice.

**`combat-finishers.schema.json`** (step 4; the rows already pass):
- Nothing in the schema. Beat args are open, so `volley` (on a cue), `gait` and `path` (on `finRush`), `slot` (on `strike` and `finalBlow`), `limb` and `shape` (on `finalBlow`) pass today. If Tools wants them typed: `volley` is `{dmg, shape, count}`; `gait` is `walk`; `path` is `spiral`; `slot` is link, heavy, ender, flurry or blast.
- A row's `fighter` and the keys of `select.byFighter` should be checked against `data/fighters/roster.json` once the split lands (today nothing checks them: the stand-ins `PROTAGONIST` and `RIVAL` pass).

**`combat-brawl.schema.json` and `combat-agency.schema.json`** (step 5, new files `combat.brawl/1` and `combat.agency/1`):
- Ops for the op list: `knockBack` (live in the director since the agency slices, args `w` and the new `half`), `hold` (`both`, `dur`), `drop` (`who`, `what`).
- Tempo names: `linkLoad`, `tradeHalf`, `checkHold`, `recoil`, `bind`, `shove`, `breakOff`, `advantage`.
- Strike args: `slot`; in `o` (closed today): `push`, `checked`, `clash`, `whiff` (booleans), `trade` (`cross`), `react` (0 to 1.6).
- Cues: `check`, `clash_light`, `slip`, `over_commit`, and `slide_brace`, which the director already emits but the cue vocabulary in `finishers.json` does not list.
- `brawl.json`: `tempo`, `launchVectors`, `endings`, `tradeBeats`, `lightsAgainstAHeavy`. `agency.json`: `blowForBlow` (rules, locations, travel, poses, camera, legal) and `ranged` (bands, midLunge, farTaunt, lightCharge, heavyCharge, meeting). Both are drafts until Encounter's A6 and A7 fix what they read.
