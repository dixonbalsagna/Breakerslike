Things to try

Attack from any distance: the director closes the gap. Tap your light key while the defender's strike winds up to parry. Tap attack again right after a hit to chase. Switch to escape in a forest, the ocean or the mountains to hide and recover, then strike from cover for an ambush bonus. As KAI, watch what happens when you launch someone near the city; VORR feeds on the wreckage.
