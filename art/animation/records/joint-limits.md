# Joint limits: baked poses that changed (2026-10-01)

Provenance record for the joint-limit fix (docs/animation/joint-limits.md). No pose sketch or key set was edited: the baked shape of a pose changed where the old bake left a knee or an elbow past its limit or bent the wrong way (thigh or upper arm twisted 100 to 180 degrees, a hinge folded past its end, a bend plane chosen by a point in the world). Everything else is as it was. Each row: the largest move of a hand or a foot ("ends", model units, feet at y = 0, about 85 tall) and of an elbow or a knee ("joints"), old bake against new. A moved end is a hand or foot target that no legal bend could reach as authored (a hand behind its own shoulder, a foot above the hip of a tuck) or a joint the clamp had to bring in; contact poses keep their contact points except where noted. Originality is unchanged: no pose is derived from anything outside this project (the `_orig` line of each pose stays the authority).

150 of 355 baked poses moved by more than 2 units at an end or 3 at a joint; the rest moved by less. Poses that did not change at all are not listed.
