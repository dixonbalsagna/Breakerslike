# The path: rounds 1 to 6

Orb's brief: stylized close-ups of the fighters' faces that are memorable and recognizable. Three directions: **A** the mask as a face (lit eyes, brows and mouth on the mask), **B** the mask partly off or broken (a real face framed by the mask), **C** no mask (a full stylized face). Each round's folder has the sheets and the engine that drew them (`engine.snapshot.mjs`). The test every round: memorable and recognizable at a glance, four fighters never confused, expression readable at 120 px.

| Round | What was drawn | Criticism | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Anti-hero's three sheets (the others' round-1 sheets were drawn afterwards from the snapshot) | His hair read as a backwards baseball cap; the eyes were small; the slash sat on the eye like a tear; the faces were generic | Sleeker hair with a long tail falling from behind, bigger eyes, thicker brows, the slash moved to the cheek |
| 2 | The Anti-hero's three sheets, revised: sleeker hair with a long tail, bigger eyes, thicker brows, the slash on the cheek (A reads well: a dark mask, lit eyes, the tail) | The dark mask was lost against the dark hair; B's break did not glow; C's faces were ordinary; the four skin tones were too alike | A lit edge on the mask, heavy lids for the Anti-hero, four distinct skin tones, a lit crack along B's break |
| 3 | All four fighters, all three directions, for the first time (the Anti-hero's sheets carry the round-3 changes) | The Protagonist's hair read as a beanie and his eyes were bug-eyed; the Empress's headband read as a karate band and her pale lit eyes vanished on the bone mask; the Cyborg's steel half was too pale | New Protagonist hair, an Empress diadem, dark eyes on the pale masks, darker steel |
| 4 | Fixes: a new Protagonist swept hair with a long tail and a widow's peak; the Empress's diadem with a centre point and the three chevrons inlaid; dark eyes on the pale masks; a darker steel | B was strongest for the Anti-hero but broke for the others: the Protagonist's lifted mask read as a shower cap and the Empress's eye cutouts read as sunglasses | Redesign B per fighter, so each mask breaks its own way |
| 5 | B redesigned: a vertical lit crack (Anti-hero), a diagonal chunk (Protagonist), a veil over the lower face (Empress), a display half (Cyborg); the damage stages added (the mask retreats, cracks, scuffs, a cut, a swollen eye) | The Empress's veil read as a white beard | Change the Empress's break to a jaw plate on the lower left |
| 6 | The candidate set: all three directions for all four fighters, the damage ladder, the blank-mask option (a masked half with no eye or mouth shapes) and the one-page comparison | The set is ready for Orb; see `docs/art/closeup-directions.md` | |
| 7 | B as the lead. Rejected ideas worth knowing: the dome mask lifted onto the head (a shower cap), eye cutouts as dark ovals (sunglasses), a lower-face veil with a pointed edge (a beard), a standing helmet-like fringe (a cap).
